The same checklist, every time

Our teams work off a fixed room-by-room checklist, so a clean in March looks like a clean in November. Anything outside the checklist gets quoted before we touch it.

  • Kitchen surfaces and sink
  • Bathrooms, shower and toilet
  • Vacuum and mop all floors
  • Dusting, sills and surfaces
  • Bins emptied and relined
  • Beds made on request

Not included as standard: inside the oven, windows, and carpet steam — all available as add-ons above.

Cleaning questions

Do I need to be home?

No. Most customers leave a key or a lockbox code on the booking. We photograph the finished job so you can check it before you're back.

Will my bond clean pass the agent's inspection?

We clean to the standard REIV agents inspect against, and if the agent flags something we come back within 72 hours at no charge.

Do you bring your own products and equipment?

Everything's included. If you'd prefer we use your products for allergy reasons, note it on the booking.

Can I get the same cleaner each visit?

For recurring bookings, yes — that's the default. We only swap when someone's away, and we tell you beforehand.
