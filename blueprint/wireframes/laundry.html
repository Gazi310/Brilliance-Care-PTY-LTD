Laundry questions

How do you count a "load"?

A load is up to 8kg — roughly a full domestic washing machine, or two large laundry bags. If you go over, we tell you before we wash and it's charged at the same rate.

What if something is damaged?

We're fully insured. Flag it within 48 hours of delivery and we'll repair, replace or refund. In six years it's been a handful of items, all sorted.

Can I use my own detergent?

Yes — leave it with the bags and note it on your booking. No charge either way.

Do you do commercial laundry?

We do — cafés, salons, short-stay properties and gyms. Get in touch for a volume rate.
