Booking & slots

How far ahead can I book?

Up to eight weeks. Slots open on a rolling basis and the busiest windows — weekday evenings and Saturday mornings — usually go about a week out.

Do laundry and cleaning share a calendar?

No. Each service line has its own availability, because they're run by different teams. You'll see separate slot pickers if you book both in one order.

Do I need an account?

No. You can book as a guest with just a phone number and address. Creating an account keeps your order history and invoices in one place.

Can I reschedule?

Free of charge up to 12 hours before your pickup window, from your orders page.

Pricing & payment

Why is it an estimate and not a fixed price?

Because nobody can weigh a laundry basket over the phone. We estimate fairly, take a 50% deposit, then invoice for the real job. Full explanation here.

When is the balance charged?

After we've assessed the job and before delivery. You can pay online from the invoice, or by card or cash to the driver on the doorstep.

What if the final invoice is less than my deposit?

We refund the difference to your original payment method within three business days.

Are prices GST inclusive?

Yes. Every price shown on the site includes GST and is in Australian dollars.

Is there a delivery fee?

Not on laundry or cleaning bookings — pickup and delivery is free across the service area. Shop-only orders carry a flat delivery fee, shown before you pay.

Laundry

How long does it take?

48 hours from pickup as standard. Express 24-hour wash is $34.99 a load if you need it sooner.

How much is a load?

Up to 8kg — about a full domestic machine, or two large bags. Over that and we tell you before washing.

Do you separate colours?

Always, along with fabric type and wash temperature. Anything questionable gets set aside and flagged rather than guessed at.

Can you handle dry-clean-only items?

Yes, $8.99 an item. Suits, coats and formalwear go to our dry-cleaning partner and come back on hangers.

Cleaning

Do I need to be home?

No — leave a key or lockbox code on the booking. We photograph the finished job so you can check it remotely.

Will a bond clean pass inspection?

We clean to the standard agents inspect against. If something's flagged we return within 72 hours at no charge.

Do you bring products and equipment?

Everything's included. Prefer we use yours for allergy reasons? Note it on the booking.

Can I get the same cleaner each time?

For recurring bookings that's the default. We only swap when someone's away, and we tell you first.

Insurance & safety

Are your staff police-checked?

Every team member, before their first job. Certificates are available on request — property managers ask for these regularly.

What insurance do you carry?

$20m public liability, plus cover for items in our care. A certificate of currency is available on request.

What if something is damaged or lost?

Report it within 48 hours of delivery. We repair, replace or refund. It's rare, and we don't argue about it.
