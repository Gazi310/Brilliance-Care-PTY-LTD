Questions we get a lot

How does the deposit work?

You pay 50% when you book, based on your estimate. Once we've collected and assessed the real job we send a final invoice for the balance — which is often less than the estimate. You settle that on delivery.

How long does laundry take?

Standard turnaround is 48 hours from pickup. Express 24-hour wash is available if you need it sooner.

Which suburbs do you cover?

More than 30 suburbs across Melbourne's east. Check your postcode above for an instant answer.

Are your products safe for sensitive skin?

Yes. We use low-tox, fragrance-free detergents as standard, and you can flag allergies on your booking.

Can I book a regular fortnightly clean?

You can. Recurring bookings keep the same team where possible, and you're charged per visit rather than up front.
